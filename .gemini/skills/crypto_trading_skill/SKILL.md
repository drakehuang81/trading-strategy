## Session-Based Trading: AMD Model

The **AMD (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution)** model is a powerful framework for understanding how price moves throughout the trading day. This is the foundation of session-based scalping.

### Session Times (UTC+8 / Taipei Time)

| Session | Time (UTC+8) | Time (UTC) | Characteristics |
|---------|--------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Asia Session** | 08:00 - 15:00 | 00:00 - 07:00 | Low volatility, range-bound, consolidation |
| **London Session** | 15:00 - 00:00 | 07:00 - 16:00 | High volatility, breakouts, liquidity hunts |
| **New York Session** | 21:00 - 06:00 | 13:00 - 22:00 | Trend continuation, follow-through moves |
| **London/NY Overlap** | 21:00 - 00:00 | 13:00 - 16:00 | **Highest volatility**, best scalping window |

### Phase 1: Accumulation (Asia Session)
**What Happens:**
- Price moves in a **tight range** (consolidation).
- Smart Money is **building positions** quietly.
- Retail traders are mostly inactive.
- Volume is low; volatility is minimal.

**What to Do:**
- [ ] **Identify the Asia Range**: Mark the **Asia Session High (ASH)** and **Asia Session Low (ASL)**.
- [ ] Do NOT trade during this phase. Wait for London.
- [ ] Note where liquidity is building (equal highs/lows within the range).

**How to Mark Asia Range:**
1. At the end of Asia Session (around 15:00 UTC+8), draw horizontal lines at:
   - **ASH (Asia Session High)**: The highest point during Asia.
   - **ASL (Asia Session Low)**: The lowest point during Asia.
2. These levels become **liquidity targets** for London.

### Phase 2: Manipulation (London Open)
**What Happens:**
- London opens with a **liquidity hunt**.
- Price often **sweeps the Asia High or Low** (or both!).
- This is the "fake move" designed to trap retail traders.
- Smart Money takes out stops before reversing.

**What to Look For:**
| Scenario | What It Looks Like | Action |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| **Bullish Day** | Price sweeps ASL (stops out longs), then reverses sharply upward | Wait for sweep, then look for Long entry |
| **Bearish Day** | Price sweeps ASH (stops out shorts), then reverses sharply downward | Wait for sweep, then look for Short entry |
| **Double Sweep** | Both ASL and ASH get taken, leaves traders confused | Wait for 15m structure shift, then trade the direction |

**The London Killzone:**
- **Best trading window**: First 2-3 hours of London (15:00 - 18:00 UTC+8).
- This is when manipulation occurs.
- Once the sweep and reversal happen, enter with SMC confirmation.

### Phase 3: Distribution (New York Session)
**What Happens:**
- The "real move" unfolds.
- Price follows through in the direction established after the London manipulation.
- This is trend continuation, not reversal.

**What to Do:**
- [ ] **Trade with the trend** that was established after London manipulation.
- [ ] Use London's reversal point as a reference. If it holds, add to position.
- [ ] Take profits during NY session as the move extends.
- [ ] Be cautious near NY close (06:00 UTC+8) as liquidity decreases.

**NY Killzone:**
- **Best trading window**: First 2 hours of NY open (21:00 - 23:00 UTC+8).
- This is when big moves continue.
- Avoid trading after 03:00 UTC+8 as volume drops.

### How to Trade AMD (Step-by-Step)

#### Morning Prep (Before 15:00 UTC+8)
1. **Mark the Asia Range** (ASH and ASL).
2. **Identify HTF Bias** (4H/1H direction).
3. **Mark SMC POIs** (Order Blocks, FVGs near Asia Range).
4. **Set Alerts** at ASH and ASL levels.

#### London Open (15:00 - 18:00 UTC+8)
1. **Watch for the Sweep**: Is price taking out ASH or ASL?
2. **Wait for Rejection**: Don't enter during the sweep. Wait for:
   - 15m candle confirmation (engulfing, pin bar).
   - LTF structure shift (CHoCH on 5m/1m).
3. **Enter with Confluence**: SMC POI + RSI/MACD confirmation.
4. **Set SL below/above the sweep wick**.

#### NY Session (21:00 - 03:00 UTC+8)
1. **Manage the Trade**: If in profit, move SL to BE.
2. **Add to Winners**: If London's reversal point holds, consider adding.
3. **Take Profits**: Scale out at TP1, TP2, TP3.
4. **Don't Overtrade**: One good AMD trade per day is enough.

## Core Skills

### 1. Multi-Timeframe Analysis (Top-Down Approach)
Always analyze from higher to lower timeframes:
1.  **4H/1H**: Determine the macro bias (Bullish / Bearish / Ranging).
2.  **15m**: Identify the Point of Interest (POI) for entry.
3.  **5m/1m** (Optional): Fine-tune entry for tighter stop loss.

### 2. SMC Structure Identification
You must accurately identify the following SMC concepts:

| Concept | Description | How to Use |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| **BOS (Break of Structure)** | Price breaks a significant high/low, confirming trend continuation. | Confirms the current trend direction. Look for longs after bullish BOS. |
| **CHoCH (Change of Character)** | The first break against the trend, signaling potential reversal. | Early warning of trend shift. Wait for confirmation before acting. |
| **Order Block (OB)** | The last bearish candle before a bullish move (for bullish OB). Represents institutional buying/selling zones. | Primary entry zone for longs. Look for price to return to the OB for a discounted entry. |
| **Fair Value Gap (FVG/Imbalance)** | A 3-candle pattern where the wicks of candle 1 and 3 do not overlap, leaving a "gap". | Price often returns to fill these gaps. Can act as a target or entry confluence. |
| **Liquidity Pool (EQH/EQL)** | Equal Highs/Lows or clustered stop losses. Smart money targets these for liquidity. | Anticipate a **liquidity sweep** (fake breakout) before the real move. |
| **Inducement** | A minor structural break designed to trap traders before the real move. | Avoid getting trapped. Wait for the sweep and reclaim. |
| **Mitigation Block** | A breaker block that has been tested and held. | Strong entry zone after the initial break and retest. |

### 3. Risk Management Calculation
**Capital preservation is paramount.**

| Parameter | Guideline |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Risk per Trade** | 0.5% - 1% of total account balance. Never exceed 2%. |
| **Risk-to-Reward (R:R)** | Minimum 1:2. Ideal is 1:3 or higher for scalps. |
| **Leverage** | Max 5x-10x for scalping. Lower leverage = more room for error. |
| **Position Sizing Formula** | `Position Size = (Account Balance * Risk %) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price)` |
| **Max Concurrent Trades** | 1-2 open positions at a time to manage focus and risk. |

### 4. Market Sentiment Interpretation (Confluence Filters)
Before entering a trade, check the following:

| Indicator | Bullish Signal | Bearish Signal |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Funding Rate** | Negative or near zero (shorts are paying longs) | Highly positive (longs are overleveraged) |
| **Open Interest (OI)** | Rising OI with rising price (new money entering longs) | Rising OI with falling price (shorts building) |
| **Liquidation Heatmap** | Large liquidation cluster above current price (magnet for price) | Large cluster below (potential target for sweep) |
| **BTC Dominance** | Falling BTC.D is bullish for altcoins | Rising BTC.D is bearish for altcoins |

### 5. Technical Indicator Confluence (RSI & MACD Resonance)
Use RSI and MACD as **confirmation tools**, not primary entry signals. SMC structure takes priority.

#### RSI (Relative Strength Index) - Settings: 14 Period
| Scenario | Long Signal | Short Signal |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Oversold/Overbought** | RSI < 30 (Oversold) + Price at Demand Zone | RSI > 70 (Overbought) + Price at Supply Zone |
| **Hidden Divergence** | Price makes Higher Low, RSI makes Lower Low → Bullish continuation | Price makes Lower High, RSI makes Higher High → Bearish continuation |
| **Regular Divergence** | Price makes Lower Low, RSI makes Higher Low → Bullish reversal | Price makes Higher High, RSI makes Lower High → Bearish reversal |
| **Mid-line Cross** | RSI crossing above 50 confirms bullish momentum | RSI crossing below 50 confirms bearish momentum |

#### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) - Settings: 12, 26, 9
| Scenario | Long Signal | Short Signal |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Signal Line Cross** | MACD line crosses ABOVE signal line | MACD line crosses BELOW signal line |
| **Zero Line Cross** | MACD crosses above 0 (bullish momentum) | MACD crosses below 0 (bearish momentum) |
| **Histogram Momentum** | Histogram bars growing (green/increasing) | Histogram bars shrinking (red/decreasing) |
| **Divergence** | Price Lower Low + MACD Higher Low = Bullish | Price Higher High + MACD Lower High = Bearish |

#### RSI + MACD Resonance Checklist
For **maximum confluence**, look for these conditions to align:

**🟢 LONG Entry Resonance:**
- [ ] RSI < 40 (preferably < 30) OR bullish divergence forming
- [ ] RSI crossing back above 30 (if was oversold)
- [ ] MACD histogram turning positive OR signal line bullish cross imminent
- [ ] MACD line is above signal line OR about to cross
- [ ] Both indicators aligning with SMC POI (Order Block / FVG)

**🔴 SHORT Entry Resonance:**
- [ ] RSI > 60 (preferably > 70) OR bearish divergence forming
- [ ] RSI crossing back below 70 (if was overbought)
- [ ] MACD histogram turning negative OR signal line bearish cross imminent
- [ ] MACD line is below signal line OR about to cross
- [ ] Both indicators aligning with SMC POI (Supply Zone / Bearish OB)

> ⚠️ **Important**: RSI/MACD alone are NOT enough. They must confirm the SMC structure, not replace it.

### 6. Fibonacci Retracement (斐波那契回撤)
Fibonacci levels help identify **Premium/Discount Zones** and potential reversal areas.

#### Key Levels & Their Meaning
| Level | Name | Usage |
|-------|------|-------|
| **0.0** | Swing Low (for bullish) / Swing High (for bearish) | Starting point of the move |
| **0.236** | Shallow Retracement | Weak pullback, strong momentum continuation |
| **0.382** | Moderate Retracement | Common pullback in strong trends |
| **0.5** | **Equilibrium (50%)** | Dividing line between Premium and Discount zones |
| **0.618** | **Golden Ratio** | Most watched level, high probability reversal zone |
| **0.705** | Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) | ICT's preferred entry zone (0.62-0.79) |
| **0.786** | Deep Retracement | Last chance for trend continuation |
| **1.0** | Swing High (for bullish) / Swing Low (for bearish) | End of the measured move |

#### Premium vs Discount Zone
| Zone | Fib Range | For Longs | For Shorts |
|------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Discount Zone** | 0.5 - 1.0 (below equilibrium) | ✅ Ideal buy zone | ❌ Avoid shorting here |
| **Premium Zone** | 0.0 - 0.5 (above equilibrium) | ❌ Avoid buying here | ✅ Ideal sell zone |

#### How to Draw Fibonacci
**For Bullish Setup (Looking for Longs):**
1. Identify the most recent significant **Swing Low** → This is your 1.0 (100%)
2. Draw to the most recent **Swing High** → This is your 0.0 (0%)
3. Look for price to retrace into the **0.618 - 0.786 zone** (OTE)
4. Combine with Order Block or FVG in this zone for confluence

**For Bearish Setup (Looking for Shorts):**
1. Identify the most recent significant **Swing High** → This is your 1.0 (100%)
2. Draw to the most recent **Swing Low** → This is your 0.0 (0%)
3. Look for price to retrace into the **0.618 - 0.786 zone** (OTE)
4. Combine with Supply Zone or Bearish OB in this zone for confluence

#### Fibonacci Extension (For Targets)
| Level | Usage |
|-------|-------|
| **-0.272** | Conservative TP1 |
| **-0.618** | Standard TP2 |
| **-1.0** | Extended TP3 (equal move) |
| **-1.618** | Aggressive TP4 |

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: The best setups occur when a Fib level (0.618/0.705) aligns with an Order Block AND shows RSI/MACD confluence.

### 7. Volume Profile & CVD (Advanced Confluence)
Volume analysis adds another layer of confirmation. Use these as **bonus confluence**, not requirements.

#### Volume Profile
| Concept | Description | How to Use |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| **POC (Point of Control)** | Price level with the highest traded volume | Acts as a magnet; price often returns to POC |
| **Value Area High (VAH)** | Upper boundary of 70% volume | Resistance zone; good for shorts if rejected |
| **Value Area Low (VAL)** | Lower boundary of 70% volume | Support zone; good for longs if held |
| **Low Volume Node (LVN)** | Price levels with little trading activity | Price moves quickly through these; don't expect support/resistance |
| **High Volume Node (HVN)** | Price levels with heavy trading activity | Strong support/resistance; price may consolidate here |

#### CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta)
| Scenario | Long Signal | Short Signal |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| **CVD Rising + Price Rising** | Healthy uptrend, buyers in control | N/A |
| **CVD Falling + Price Falling** | N/A | Healthy downtrend, sellers in control |
| **CVD Divergence (Bullish)** | Price makes Lower Low, CVD makes Higher Low → Hidden buying | Caution for shorts |
| **CVD Divergence (Bearish)** | Caution for longs | Price makes Higher High, CVD makes Lower High → Hidden selling |
| **CVD Spike at Support** | Large positive delta spike = aggressive buying | N/A |
| **CVD Spike at Resistance** | N/A | Large negative delta spike = aggressive selling |

> ⚠️ **Note**: Volume Profile and CVD require additional tools (e.g., Exocharts, Coinalyze, TradingView Premium). They are optional but powerful.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid ❌

Learn from these common errors to improve your win rate:

### 1. Entry Mistakes
| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | How to Fix |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| **Entering at POI without confirmation** | POI is a zone, not a trigger. Price may sweep through it. | Wait for LTF structure shift (CHoCH/BOS) or candle confirmation. |
| **Chasing price after missing entry** | Entering late ruins R:R and increases risk. | Accept the miss. There's always another setup. |
| **Trading against HTF trend** | Lower probability, higher risk. | Only trade in the direction of 4H/1H bias. |
| **Ignoring RSI/MACD divergence** | Divergence warns of momentum exhaustion. | If divergence contradicts setup, reduce size or skip. |
| **Forcing trades in chop/range** | Sideways markets have low probability. | Wait for clear structure. Trade the breakout, not the range. |

### 2. Risk Management Mistakes
| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | How to Fix |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| **No stop loss** | One bad trade can wipe out weeks of gains. | ALWAYS set SL before entering. No exceptions. |
| **Moving stop loss further away** | Increases loss, breaks risk management. | Accept original SL. If it's hit, you were wrong. |
| **Overleveraging** | 20x+ leverage = liquidation risk. | Max 5-10x for scalps. Capital preservation > quick gains. |
| **Risking more than 1-2% per trade** | Drawdowns become unrecoverable. | Stick to 0.5-1% risk per trade. |
| **Averaging down a losing trade** | Hoping ≠ Trading. This is gambling. | If SL is hit, exit. Don't add to losers. |

### 3. Exit Mistakes
| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | How to Fix |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| **Taking profit too early** | Leaves money on the table, hurts R:R. | Use partial TPs (30/30/40 split). Let winners run. |
| **Not moving SL to break even** | Winning trade can become a loser. | Move SL to BE after TP1 is hit. |
| **Holding through major news** | FOMC/CPI can cause unexpected volatility. | Close or reduce position before high-impact events. |
| **Revenge trading after a loss** | Emotional state leads to poor decisions. | Take a break. Review the loss objectively. |

### 4. Analysis Mistakes
| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | How to Fix |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| **Only looking at one timeframe** | Miss the bigger picture. | Always do top-down analysis (4H → 1H → 15m). |
| **Ignoring Fibonacci zones** | Miss optimal entry areas. | Draw Fib on every setup. Look for 0.618-0.786 confluence. |
| **Over-relying on indicators** | Indicators lag; SMC structures lead. | SMC first, then use RSI/MACD for confirmation. |
| **Confirmation bias** | Only seeing what you want to see. | Actively look for reasons NOT to take the trade. |

## Trading Psychology 🧠

Technical skill is only 20% of trading success. The other 80% is **mindset and discipline**.

### Core Principles

#### 1. Accept That Losses Are Part of the Game
- Even the best traders have a 50-60% win rate.
- A loss is not a failure—it's the cost of doing business.
- Focus on **R:R**, not win rate. You can be profitable with 40% win rate if R:R is 1:3.

#### 2. Detach from Money
- Think in **R (risk units)**, not dollars.
- "I made 3R today" is healthier than "I made $300."
- This reduces emotional attachment to individual trades.

#### 3. Process Over Outcome
| Good Process, Bad Outcome | Bad Process, Good Outcome |
|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Followed all rules, but SL hit. **This is OK.** | Broke rules, but got lucky. **This is dangerous.** |
| Learn nothing new; you did everything right. | Learn nothing new; luck won't last. |

#### 4. The 3-Strike Rule
- **If you lose 3 trades in a row, STOP TRADING for the day.**
- You're either:
  - In a bad market condition, OR
  - In a bad mental state.
- Take a break. Review your trades. Come back tomorrow.

#### 5. Pre-Trade Routine
Before every session:
- [ ] Review your trading plan and rules.
- [ ] Check the economic calendar for news.
- [ ] Assess your mental state (tired? stressed? revenge-trading?).
- [ ] Set a max loss limit for the day (e.g., 2% of account).

### Emotional Management

| Emotion | Trigger | Solution |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **FOMO** | Seeing a move without you | There's always another trade. Quality > Quantity. |
| **Greed** | Winning streak, want more | Stick to your TP plan. Don't let winners become losers. |
| **Fear** | Recent losses, hesitation | If setup is valid, take it. Trust your process. |
| **Revenge** | Just lost, want to recover | STOP. Take a break. Revenge trading = more losses. |
| **Overconfidence** | Winning streak | Stay humble. The market doesn't care about your streak. |

### Daily Journaling
Keep a trading journal with:
- **Date/Time**
- **Pair traded**
- **Direction (Long/Short)**
- **Entry/SL/TP levels**
- **R:R achieved**
- **Screenshot of the setup**
- **What went well / What to improve**
- **Emotional state before/during/after**

> 📝 **Review your journal weekly.** Patterns will emerge. Learn from them.

### The 10 Commandments of Scalping
1. **Protect capital first.** Profits come second.
2. **No setup = No trade.** Patience is a skill.
3. **Plan the trade, trade the plan.** No improvising.
4. **Cut losses quickly.** Let winners run.
5. **Never risk more than you can afford to lose.**
6. **The market is always right.** Your opinion doesn't matter.
7. **Revenge trading is the fastest way to blow your account.**
8. **One good trade a day is enough.**
9. **If in doubt, stay out.**
10. **Live to trade another day.**
